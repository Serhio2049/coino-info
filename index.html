What is CoinoAxis?

CoinoAxis is an educational initiative created by the Coino development team. It helps beginners and professionals understand decentralized technologies.

  • Courses from basics to advanced
  • Community of like‑minded people
  • Hands‑on tasks and hackathons
  • Certification upon completion

Goal: Train 10,000 specialists by 2027.
